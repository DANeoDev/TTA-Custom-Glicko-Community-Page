Guide: Step 1 of 5 • Rating Engines
⚡ The Three Competitive Engines

Through the Ages features three distinct statistical algorithms to measure player skill:

  • Glicko-2 Standard: Naive 1v1 pairwise decomposition without multiplayer downweighting (matches the legacy digital app baseline).
  • Glicko-2 MP-Weighted: Uses Coulom fractional weighting ($w = 1/(N-1)$) so a 4-player game generates 1 game worth of rating variance rather than 3, preventing overconfidence.
  • Whole-History Rating (WHR): Rémi Coulom's retrospective Bayesian state-space model that optimizes career skill over time with daily Brownian motion drift ($w^2 \Delta t$).

Guide: Step 2 of 5 • Competitive Formats
👥 Player Counts & Circuits

TTA tournaments span different table sizes with wildly different tactical dynamics:

  • 4-Player: International Championship premier pyramid. Heavy diplomatic coalitions and split military focus.
  • 3-Player: Intermezzo Championship. Balanced triangle warfare and high tactical density.
  • 2-Player (Duel): Royal League. Zero-sum head-to-head tactical warfare.
  • All Formats: Global combined ratings across every official match.
Guide: Step 3 of 5 • Season Resets
⚙️ Golden Ratio Season Resets

Reset modes prevent ratings from stagnating across multiple calendar years using exact powers of the Golden Ratio ($\phi \approx 1.618034$):

  • Continuous (Career): Standard uninterrupted trajectory without annual boundary resets.
  • Soft Reset ($\phi$): $\mathrm{RD} \cdot \phi$ uncertainty expansion & tier shrinkage $\lambda = \phi^{-3} \approx 23.61\%$ toward divisional means.
  • Amplified Reset ($\phi^2$): $\mathrm{RD} \cdot \phi^2$ uncertainty expansion & amplified tier shrinkage $\lambda = \phi^{-1} \approx 61.80\%$.
Guide: Step 4 of 5 • Deltas & Ratings
📈 Conservative Rating & Deltas

How leaderboard ranks and rating changes are computed:

  • Conservative Rating ($C = \mu - 2\mathrm{RD}$): Ranks players by their 95% statistical lower bound. This ensures inactive players with high uncertainty don't block the summit.
  • Webmaster Deltas: View exact rating gains ($\Delta$) since the last tournament batch was committed to the platform.
  • Seasonal Stats Switcher: In any historical season, toggle match records between that season only or career-to-date.
