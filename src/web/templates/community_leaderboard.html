📜 The Best-8 Rolling Point System

The Community Standard Leaderboard ranks players by cumulative performance across the global circuit. To reward peak mastery while preventing burnout, rankings operate on a strict Best-8 Rolling Rule:

  • Top 8 Scores Count: A player's total is the sum of their 8 highest-scoring tournament finishes across all 19 active event slots.
  • Participation Protected: If a competitor has played in 8 or fewer events, all scores sum normally. Any scores beyond the 8th are dropped—ensuring players can freely compete without risking their placement.
  • Visual Cues: In the table, counting scores are highlighted in gold boxes, while dropped scores are faded.

🏆 Tracked Event Slots & Point Tiers

The leaderboard covers 19 major tournaments over a rolling 1-to-2 year seasonal window:

  • World Championship (wrld): 2,500 pts max • The premier global biennial championship (won by a440 in 2025).
  • International Championship (ic_30–33): 1,000 pts max per season • 4 rolling seasons of the classic 4-player league.
  • Intermezzo Championship (im_27–30): 1,000 pts max per season • 4 rolling seasons of the 3-player championship.
  • Royal League (rl_05–08): 1,000 pts max per season • 4 rolling seasons of elite divisional play.
  • Grand Slams & Ladders: Wimbledon, Australian Open, Sodium Ladder, Mercurial Ladder (1,000 pts max each), Eiffel Cup & Quick & Dirty (600 pts).
