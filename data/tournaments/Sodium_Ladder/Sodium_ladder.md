Welcome to Sodium Ladder!
Discord channel for news and updates

Sodium Ladder is a sibling tournament to Mercurial Ladder, at least you could be salty when the results appeared to be not so promising for you...
It's a perpitual league with just one 4-player game per round and dynamic definition of tier you belong to.

Each player belongs to a tier which is defined as a sum of 2 components:

Result points as a result of all previous rounds: 1st place adds 3 result points, 2nd place adds 1 point, 3rd place subtracts 1 point, while last place subtracts 3 points. Exception: 1st/2nd place in the highest tier game adds only 1.5/0.5 points.
Rating points which are based on the latest available Glicko rating according to next formula:
Rating points = 10 + MAX (0; ROUNDUP ((Conservative Rating - 1000) / 20))
Groups contain players of the closest possible tiers. 

You can join, leave and re-join at any time!
Sodium Ladder has two rounds per quarter. It approximately starts 2 weeks after the last batch of both International and Intermezzo Championships.
It is Random Mix medium async tournament with 36h reserve time per Age.

Tie-brakers for seeding puproses are:

Result points
Latest Global Glicko rating
All seeding details and results could be found at Google Sheet.

Sodium Ladder games contribute to Glicko rating managed by ruby_buby.
Latest 8 rounds of Sodium Ladder count as Yellow-tier 1000-points tournament to TTA Leaderboard managed by wolvs.
