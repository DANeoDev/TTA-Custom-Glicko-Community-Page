The Transcontinental Ladder is a perpetual 3-player competition. SIxty-six players compete in 22 Rungs. First place gets promoted to the next highest-level Rung; 3rd place gets relegated down to the next-lowest Rung, 2nd place stays put.

Each round, the winner of Rung 1 is crowned Champion and added (or updated) on the Honor Roll of Champions. 

The Fine Print:

Tiebreaker is player's rank in the ladder, which is top to bottom in the rankings sheet.

Please no table talk in the chat room. Do not discuss pacts before offering them, for instance.

Be nice. 

Resignation is always preferable to timing out or abandoment

Timeouts and abandonments are not tolerated. Any timeout or abandonment will result in permanent removal from the TCL.

If you think you might timeout, please resign instead. Resignation will preserve your place in the TCL.

Do not rely on the app's notification system to tell you when it's your turn. If you can't be bothered to check the app once a day, then perhaps a serious competitive environment is not for you. 

Most players enter the ladder at the bottom. A returing player may be offered entry at a higher rung if one is available.

Two consecutive 3rd place finishes in the bottom rung will result in relegation to the bottom of the waiting list.
