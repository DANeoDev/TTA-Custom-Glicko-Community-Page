Welcome to Mercurial Ladder!
Discord channel for news and updates

It's a league with just only one 3-player game per season and dynamic definition of tier you belong (that really makes it mercurial!)

Each player belongs to a tier which is defined as a sum of 2 components:

Result points as a result of all previous seasons: 1st place adds 1 result point, 2nd place changes nothing, 3rd place substracts 1 result point. Exception: 1st place in the highest tier game adds only 0.5 points.
Rating points which are based on the latest available Glicko rating according to next formula:
Rating points = 1 + MAX (0; ROUNDUP ((Conservative Rating - 1000) / 50))
Groups contain players of the same tier. 

You can join, leave and re-join at any time!
New seasons will start on Sundays one full week after the last game of previous season has finished.

Tie-brakers for seeding puproses are:

Result points
Latest Global Glicko rating
All seeding details and results could be found at Google Sheet.

Mercurial Ladder games contribute to Glicko rating managed by ruby_buby.
Latest 10 rounds of Mercurial Ladder count as Yellow-tier 1000-points tournament to TTA Leaderboard managed by wolvs.
