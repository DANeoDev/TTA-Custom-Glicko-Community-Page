G'day TTA players,

 We're starting the 2026 TTA Australian Open!

 256 players, 7 stages, 1 winner

 There are going to be 64 groups of 4 players in stage 1 - places 1 & 2 qualify for stage 2 where there will be 32 groups of 4 players, first 2 places qualify for stage 3, and so on until stage 6 when there will be 2 groups of 4 players. The players in spots 1 & 2 qualify in both groups for the Australian International Open Grand Final. The Grand Final winner will be the 2026 TTA Australian Open champion.

 As this is an annual event, planned to start on Australia Day - 26 January 2026 at 20:00 ACST.

The top 64 players in terms of Glicko rating will be seeded for the first round and arranged similarly to tennis championship tournaments from the second round with seeds No1 and 2 at both ends of the groups.

Should 2 or more players finish the game with the same number of points, the players with the most experience points shall be promoted to the next stage.

At every new stage the winners of adjacent groups (say 1 to 4) will be distributed as follows: winners of groups 1 and 2 joined by second places in groups 3 and 4, winners of groups 3 and 4 joined by second places in groups 1 and 2 and so on. This is NOT a doubles competition. Round 3 has been randomised in order to increase the competition.

 The players should exercise fair play during the matches and any abuse will not be tolerated. Should an abuse take place please take a screen pic and let me know. The player will be banned from the next edition of the tournament.

 Time-outs will not be promoted to the next level irrespective of the position of the AI replacement in the group.

 A trophy will go to the winner from the organiser.
