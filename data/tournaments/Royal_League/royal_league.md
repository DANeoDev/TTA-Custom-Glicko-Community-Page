Welcome to Royal League!

Short description:

Royal League pretends to be main 2p-only recurrent TTA tournament.
It has one 7-game season per quarter. Games are launched on weekly basis.
Divisional structure is Fibonacci-pyramidal.

Division structure:

The league consists of these divisions (the highest is on the top), each division has some number of leagues:

Emperor - 1 group
King - also 1 group
Prince - 2 groups
Duke - 3 groups
Marquess - 5 groups
Count - 8 groups
Viscount - 13 groups
Baron - 21 groups
Knight - up to 34 groups
Each group consists of 8 players.
Each player will play 7 games over approximately 8 weeks.
Every player in a group will meet everyone else exactly once. 

Timeouts are non-tolerated

One timeout per season is allowed. This timeout would count as your loss as well as your opponent's win.
Second timeout may cause disqualification. It means: 

You finish in last place in your group (that results in two-divisions down relegation)
All your unstarted games are cancelled
All your finished and running games are untied from the tournament (thus these games become just friendly games)
You will not receive invite for the next season of Royal League (but you are still allowed to participate)
Important! Disqualification is done manually (so look at the group standings to check if the player is disqualified).
All games are launched under medium async with 48h of reserve time for each Age.

Standings

Group standings are calculated by comparing the following, in order:

Points (2 for win, 1 for draw, 0 for loss)
In case of tie, players are classified by number of points in all head-to-head games. If this rule has broken some ties it applies again. 
Re-matches will be appointed in Divisions up to Marquess if the tie still left is crucial!
Promotion and relegation

Double-promotion rule. One best player across all the groups of each Prince, Duke, Marquess, and Count divisions gets double-promotion. As well double-promotions are granted for 2 best players of Viscount, 3 best players of Baron and 5 best players of Knight. Best players are ranked by total number of points (cancelled games due to disqualification count as a win), then if needed tie-breaker matches are appointed.
Other group winners and all 2nd places get standard one-division promotion.
6th and 7th places are relegated one-division down. The only exception is 5th place in King which is also relegated (5th place in King has priority to stay in case free space is available in King)
Last places are relegated two-division down.
Players who are coming back after dropping some seasons will play as closer to division of their latest results available as possible. You can join and re-join at any time when registaration is open.
High-rated / well-known newcomers may be granted with a spot at higher division.
Royal League games (including official tie-breakers) contribute to Glicko rating managed by ruby_buby.
Each season of Royal League counts as Blue-tier 1000-points tournament to TTA Leaderboard managed by wolvs.
